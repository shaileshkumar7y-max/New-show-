# BIG BOSS KE RANDIYA — BLACKED release 1.0

## What's new
- The public homepage is now the original BLACKED-branded **BIG BOSS KE RANDIYA** show experience.
- A one-time 18+ entry gate requires confirmation that every player is an adult and that boundaries will be respected.
- Twenty game modes are playable: Naughty Spin Wheel, Truth Bomb, Dare or Double, Secret Crush, Chemistry Test, Eye Contact Battle, Model Walk, Pose Challenge, Compliment Battle, Mystery Box, Who Knows Whom, Forbidden Question, Couple Sync, Temptation Test, Secret Mission, Boss Says, Hot Seat, Winner's Choice, Final Temptation and BLACKED Fashion Show.
- Three comfort levels: Flirty, Naughty and Temptation. Prompts are non-graphic and designed around conversation, confidence, fashion and mutual choice.
- Points, rounds, skips, housemates, custom prompts and history are saved locally in the browser.
- Export/import backup, share, responsive mobile navigation, PWA manifest and revised offline cache.
- The previous YaarJam static game interface is preserved at `classic.html` and still uses the existing `app.js` and `styles.css` files.

## Important boundaries
- Every challenge can be skipped, paused or stopped without penalty.
- The Off-camera Choice ends the round and leaves any private activity entirely to the players; the site does not instruct or record sexual activity.
- No nudity, forced touch, sexual acts, images, recordings or private-content sharing are required.
- No backend or analytics is added. Names, scores and custom prompts remain in local browser storage.
- The page uses an original typographic show identity; it does not use the official Bigg Boss logo or claim TV-show affiliation.

## Deploy
Keep `index.html`, `classic.html`, `manifest.json`, `icon.svg`, `sw.js`, `styles.css` and `app.js` in the repository root. Commit/push the updated files to the branch configured for GitHub Pages. The Classic Arcade files are preserved; do not delete `app.js` or `styles.css`.
